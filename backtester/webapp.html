Monte Carlo simulation

    
Simulate thousands of possible futures for a portfolio: the range of balances over time, the chance the money lasts, and the highest safe withdrawal rate.

    

    

      

    

      
A fixed-weight portfolio is simulated from its assets. Anything with rules (switches, rotations, signals) is simulated by resampling the strategy's own monthly returns.

Factor analysis

    
Explain returns with the market, size, value, profitability, investment and momentum factors (Kenneth French's data): loadings with t-stats, R² and the annualised alpha left over.

    

    

    

    

    

    

      
      
      
      
    

    

  

  

    

    
Factor loadings

      
A loading is the return's sensitivity to the factor; |t| ≥ 1.96 is significant at about 5%. Alpha is the average return the factors do not explain.
