Objectives 

      
Max return / max drawdown maximises CAGR over the worst drawdown of the historical monthly path (searched from several starting portfolios); max Omega maximises the Omega ratio (gains over losses around the threshold, solved exactly as a linear programme). An unreachable target volatility or return is reported with the reachable range. Long-history series such as SPYSIM and TLTSIM can be used as tickers when present.

Monte Carlo simulation

    
Simulate thousands of possible futures for a portfolio: the range of balances over time, the chance the money lasts, and the highest safe withdrawal rate.

    

    

      

    

      
A fixed-weight portfolio is simulated from its assets. Anything with rules (switches, rotations, signals) is simulated by resampling the strategy's own monthly returns.

Factor analysis

    
Explain returns with the market, size, value, profitability, investment and momentum factors (Kenneth French's data, US or developed markets) and the bond factors TERM (long Treasuries − T-bills) and DEF (corporates − Treasuries): loadings with t-stats, R² and the annualised alpha left over.

    

    

    

    

    

    

      
      
      
      
    

    

  

  

    

    
Factor loadings

      
A loading is the return's sensitivity to the factor; |t| ≥ 1.96 is significant at about 5%. Alpha is the average return the factors do not explain.

Asset correlations

    
The correlation of total returns (dividends reinvested) over the period the assets have in common, a rolling correlation for one pair, and each asset's statistics, like Portfolio Visualizer's asset correlations and asset analysis. Long-history series (SPYSIM, TLTSIM, EFASIM…) reach back further.
