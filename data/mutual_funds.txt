# Curated mutual funds the data job downloads (full daily NAV history with distributions, from Yahoo Finance), in
# addition to the family lists in backtester/fund_lists.py. "## Family" starts a family (the Funds page shows it as
# the category until Yahoo's metadata arrives); symbols are separated by spaces or new lines; '#' starts a comment.
# Every symbol was checked against the SEC's list of mutual fund share-class symbols (company_tickers_mf.json) and
# the fund's registrant (CIK) matched to its family; a symbol Yahoo doesn't serve is simply skipped by the data job
# (broad_failed in data/universe.json) and retried after 30 days.
# Yahoo's mutual fund histories start in January 1980 at the earliest (VFINX, launched 1976-08-31, starts 1980-01-02
# there, as does every older fund) - that is the source's limit, not the download's (see README, "Data coverage").
